The **Microbial Genomics Laboratory** at the Department of Laboratory Medicine, Yonsei University College of Medicine, aims to improve the accuracy and clinical role of laboratory diagnostics and to realize precision medicine through next-generation sequencing (NGS) technologies and AI-based predictive algorithm development.

## Vision

Through in-depth analysis of microbial genomes and the human microbiome, we develop models for accurate diagnosis, prognosis prediction, and treatment-response prediction in infectious diseases.

## Core Research Areas

- **Bacterial genomics** — Oxford Nanopore long-read whole-genome analysis
- **Microbiome research** — discovery of disease-associated biomarkers (colorectal cancer, preterm birth, etc.) and assay standardization
- **Clinical translation** — bringing state-of-the-art laboratory techniques into clinical practice
- **Bioinformatics** — large-scale sequencing data processing and standardization, and pipelines applicable across multiple institutions

## Research Infrastructure

Big-data production with large-scale whole-genome sequencing platforms

Large-scale bacterial genome pipelines operated on GPU-accelerated Linux analysis servers
