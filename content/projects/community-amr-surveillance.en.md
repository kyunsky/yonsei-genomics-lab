Advancing an integrated One Health surveillance system for antimicrobial resistance across human, animal, and environmental sectors through metagenome-based epidemiological linkage analysis

(Year 1) Specimen collection, target bacteria isolation, characterization, and metagenomic analysis focused on healthy residents, clinics, livestock farms, farming households, traditional markets, wastewater treatment plants, and rivers

(Year 2) Specimen collection, target bacteria isolation, characterization, and metagenomic analysis focused on regional long-term care hospitals and their surroundings, and veterinary hospitals and their surroundings

(Year 3) In-depth analysis of antimicrobial-resistant bacteria with possible cross-sector epidemiological links, and optimization of metagenomic approaches to advance the community-level integrated AMR surveillance system
